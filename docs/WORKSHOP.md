[h1]Missile Disaster[/h1]Launch missiles at any spot — and defend against them. Five warhead types, adjustable yield, air/ground burst, realistic blast falloff, fire, and persistent radioactive fallout. Now with an optional random-strike “disaster mode”.[b]Base game only — no DLC required.[/b] Every explosion, fireball and mushroom cloud is the mod's own rather than a vanilla effect, so each one is built to the size of the warhead that made it.[h2]Features[/h2][list][*][b]5 warheads[/b]: Conventional, Cluster, White Phosphorus, Thermobaric, Nuclear[*][b]Adjustable yield[/b]: nuclear presets (Little Boy … Tsar Bomba) or custom kt; conventional in kg TNT[*][b]Air / ground burst[/b]: ground makes a crater and destroys roads/pipes/metro; air detonates in mid-air above the target — no crater, roads/pipes/metro spared, but the blast and fires reach wider[*][b]Mushroom cloud[/b]: always rises from the ground, even after an air burst, and its canopy is as wide as the destruction radius — so the cloud shows you exactly how far the blast reached[*][b]Yield you can see[/b]: the fireball is sized from the charge, and craters are exaggerated so even a single conventional hit leaves a proper scar without flattening the whole block[*][b]True incendiary[/b]: White Phosphorus makes no crater and almost no blast at any charge — a heavier charge just spreads the fires further[*][b]Blast debris[/b]: the rubble of what stood at ground zero is swept outward as one expanding ring - solid lit chunks, not sprites - skipping along the ground and tumbling as they go, travelling with the dust instead of scattering. Car-sized whatever the yield: a bigger warhead does not make bigger masonry, it breaks more of it and pushes it further[*][b]Traffic and people are thrown[/b]: cars, trucks, buses, service vehicles and parked cars are hurled outward from ground zero and tumble to a stop, and pedestrians go with them. How far scales with the yield - a bomb shoves the cars beside it a few metres, a strategic warhead throws them a hundred. No DLC needed. Trains, planes and ships are not affected[*][b]Trees catch fire[/b] inside the thermal ring - fiercest at the centre, petering out at the edge. [i]Needs the Natural Disasters DLC: the game's own tree-burning is gated on it. Without the DLC nothing happens and the rest of the mod is unaffected.[/i][*][b]Distance falloff[/b]: total destruction at ground zero, tapering outward[*][b]Radioactive fallout[/b] from nuclear ground bursts — persists, expires after 50 in-game years (or cleaned by a Decontamination facility)[*][b]Missile defense[/b]: PAC-3 / THAAD / Aegis interceptors with realistic kill probabilities, plus a Radar boost[/list][h2]Random strikes (disaster mode) — OFF by default[/h2][b]Missiles never fall on their own unless you switch this on.[/b] It is off in a fresh install and stays off until you tick it in [b]Options → Mods → Missile Disaster[/b]. Once on, missiles hit your city automatically and destroy buildings, exactly like a natural disaster — so if your city is being flattened and you did not launch anything, that box is ticked. The first strike in each city also chirps to say so.[list][*][b]Disaster-linked frequency[/b]: the strike rate scales with the game's natural-disaster frequency, and the timer resets whenever another disaster occurs — so missiles fall in the gaps between disasters (adjustable ×0.25–3.0)[*][b]Attack pattern[/b]: Single, [b]MIRV[/b] (3–6 warheads striking the city simultaneously), or Random[*][b]Priority targeting (configurable)[/b]: favors key buildings — by default nuclear plants & interceptor sites (PAC3 / THAAD / Aegis), then airports / train stations / harbors, then landmarks & monuments. Edit the target keywords for each tier in Options (e.g. add “Oil” to target oil industry).[*][b]Warhead[/b]: random, or a fixed type[/list][h2]Required companion assets[/h2]This mod detects buildings [b]by name[/b]. Subscribe/create building assets whose names contain:[list][*][b]PAC3[/b] — terminal-tier interceptor[*][b]THAAD[/b] — mid-tier interceptor[*][b]Aegis[/b] — high-tier interceptor[*][b]Radar[/b] — boosts intercept chance[*][b]Decontamination[/b] (e.g. “Decontamination facility”) — removes fallout (~5%/in-game month)[/list][h2]How to use[/h2][olist][*]Open the [b]Disasters[/b] info-view panel and click the [b]missile icon[/b] sitting next to the vanilla disaster buttons — this opens the Missile Launch Control panel[*]Pick a warhead, set the yield, choose air or ground burst[*]Click [b]Start Targeting[/b], then click the map[/olist]Prefer a hotkey? Press the launch key ([b]default F9[/b], rebindable in Options) to re-arm targeting instantly with your current warhead settings.[h2]Options[/h2][b]Options → Missile Disaster[/b]: rebind the launch hotkey, re-open / reset the launch panel to a visible position, and configure random strikes (enable, frequency, attack pattern, warhead, and priority-target keywords).

[h1]New in this update[/h1]
[list]
[*] [b]The blast now throws the traffic and the people.[/b] Cars, trucks, buses, service vehicles and parked cars are hurled away from ground zero, tumble through the air and come to rest where they land; pedestrians and animals go too. This uses the game's own machinery - the same call the vanilla meteor makes - so nothing is being driven by hand and no DLC is required.
[*] How far they go scales with the yield: a 1 t bomb shoves the cars within about 5 m of it some 13 m, a 150 kt warhead throws them nearly 100 m and still moves cars a kilometre out, and a Tsar Bomba throws them 170 m.
[*] [i]Trains, planes and ships are not thrown.[/i] Their AI simply has no hook for it, and faking it would mean moving them by hand every frame, which is how mods corrupt save games. I would rather say so than ship that.
[/list]

[h1]Language[/h1]
English and Japanese - the game's own language setting picks between them.
Other languages are welcome. The mod ships Locales/en.txt as a template: copy it to your language code (de.txt, fr.txt, zh.txt, ...), translate the values, and open a pull request on GitHub or post the file in the comments. Missing lines fall back to English, so a partial translation is fine.
