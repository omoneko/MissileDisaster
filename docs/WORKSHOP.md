[h1]Missile Disaster[/h1]Launch missiles at any spot — and defend against them. Five warhead types, adjustable yield, air/ground burst, realistic blast falloff, fire, and persistent radioactive fallout. Now with an optional random-strike “disaster mode”.[b]Base game only — no DLC required.[/b] With the Natural Disasters DLC, the vanilla meteor effect is used for the explosion flash; otherwise a built-in fireball is used. The nuclear mushroom cloud is always the mod's own, so it can be built to the width of the blast.[h2]Features[/h2][list][*][b]5 warheads[/b]: Conventional, Cluster, White Phosphorus, Thermobaric, Nuclear[*][b]Adjustable yield[/b]: nuclear presets (Little Boy … Tsar Bomba) or custom kt; conventional in kg TNT[*][b]Air / ground burst[/b]: ground makes a crater and destroys roads/pipes/metro; air detonates in mid-air above the target — no crater, roads/pipes/metro spared, but the blast and fires reach wider[*][b]Mushroom cloud[/b]: always rises from the ground, even after an air burst, and its canopy is as wide as the destruction radius — so the cloud shows you exactly how far the blast reached[*][b]Yield you can see[/b]: the fireball is sized from the charge, and craters are exaggerated so even a single conventional hit leaves a proper scar without flattening the whole block[*][b]True incendiary[/b]: White Phosphorus makes no crater and almost no blast at any charge — a heavier charge just spreads the fires further[*][b]Blast debris[/b]: the rubble of what stood at ground zero is thrown out and up on real ballistic arcs - solid lit chunks, not sprites - tumbling and landing back across the city, with the dust it carries[*][b]Trees catch fire[/b] inside the thermal ring - fiercest at the centre, petering out at the edge. [i]Needs the Natural Disasters DLC: the game's own tree-burning is gated on it. Without the DLC nothing happens and the rest of the mod is unaffected.[/i][*][b]Distance falloff[/b]: total destruction at ground zero, tapering outward[*][b]Radioactive fallout[/b] from nuclear ground bursts — persists, expires after 50 in-game years (or cleaned by a Decontamination facility)[*][b]Missile defense[/b]: PAC-3 / THAAD / Aegis interceptors with realistic kill probabilities, plus a Radar boost[/list][h2]Random strikes (disaster mode) — OFF by default[/h2][b]Missiles never fall on their own unless you switch this on.[/b] It is off in a fresh install and stays off until you tick it in [b]Options → Mods → Missile Disaster[/b]. Once on, missiles hit your city automatically and destroy buildings, exactly like a natural disaster — so if your city is being flattened and you did not launch anything, that box is ticked. The first strike in each city also chirps to say so.[list][*][b]Disaster-linked frequency[/b]: the strike rate scales with the game's natural-disaster frequency, and the timer resets whenever another disaster occurs — so missiles fall in the gaps between disasters (adjustable ×0.25–3.0)[*][b]Attack pattern[/b]: Single, [b]MIRV[/b] (3–6 warheads striking the city simultaneously), or Random[*][b]Priority targeting (configurable)[/b]: favors key buildings — by default nuclear plants & interceptor sites (PAC3 / THAAD / Aegis), then airports / train stations / harbors, then landmarks & monuments. Edit the target keywords for each tier in Options (e.g. add “Oil” to target oil industry).[*][b]Warhead[/b]: random, or a fixed type[/list][h2]Required companion assets[/h2]This mod detects buildings [b]by name[/b]. Subscribe/create building assets whose names contain:[list][*][b]PAC3[/b] — terminal-tier interceptor[*][b]THAAD[/b] — mid-tier interceptor[*][b]Aegis[/b] — high-tier interceptor[*][b]Radar[/b] — boosts intercept chance[*][b]Decontamination[/b] (e.g. “Decontamination facility”) — removes fallout (~5%/in-game month)[/list][h2]How to use[/h2][olist][*]Open the [b]Disasters[/b] info-view panel and click the [b]missile icon[/b] sitting next to the vanilla disaster buttons — this opens the Missile Launch Control panel[*]Pick a warhead, set the yield, choose air or ground burst[*]Click [b]Start Targeting[/b], then click the map[/olist]Prefer a hotkey? Press the launch key ([b]default F9[/b], rebindable in Options) to re-arm targeting instantly with your current warhead settings.[h2]Options[/h2][b]Options → Missile Disaster[/b]: rebind the launch hotkey, re-open / reset the launch panel to a visible position, and configure random strikes (enable, frequency, attack pattern, warhead, and priority-target keywords).
